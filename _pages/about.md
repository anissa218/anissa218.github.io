Hi, I'm Anissa, a final year PhD student in Machine Learning at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/)!


I'm broadly interested in how ML models need to be adapted because of the non-i.i.d. nature of real-world data. I am therefore interested in topics including **fairness**, **generalisation**, and **data-centric ML**. 
I recently interned at [Google](https://research.google/), working on **[agents for scientific discovery](https://deepmind.google/blog/co-scientist-a-multi-agent-ai-partner-to-accelerate-research/)**, and at [Cohere](https://cohere.com/), researching **LLM safety and evaluation**. Previously, I completed my BSc at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/).


Outside of research, I spend a lot of (too much?) time exercising, particularly running, playing tennis, and skiing.


I'm always happy to connect and discuss my/your research so please reach out if any of the above sounds interesting!
