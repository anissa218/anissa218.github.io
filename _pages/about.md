Hi, I'm Anissa, a 2nd year ML PhD student at Oxford. I'm broadly interested in if and how ML models need to be adapted in the presence of non-iid training data (and non-iid, possibly shifted, test data). For my PhD so far, I have focused on biases in training data, exploring how they affect downstream models, and if bias mitigation methods can be used to tackle them (and why they often fail, see [here!](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21363)). Before my PhD I studied my BSc at Imperial College London. 
\
Outside of research, I spend a lot (too much?) time exercising, particularly running, triathlon training, playing tennis, and skiing.
